Hurt in an accident or at work?

No-Fault In a car accident?

New York No-Fault covers your physical therapy regardless of who was at fault. Bring your claim number — we bill the auto carrier directly.

Workers' Comp Hurt on the job?

Report the injury to your employer, then call us. We handle the authorizations and paperwork so you can focus on recovering.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

Do I need a referral to start?

No. You can begin treatment without a referral. Some insurance plans require one for coverage, so we verify yours before your first visit. (Confirm the no-referral policy with Dr. Iftikhar.)

How long is each appointment?

Plan on 45–60 minutes, one-on-one with your therapist. You won't be passed between aides or left on a machine.

What should I wear?

Comfortable, loose-fitting clothes you can move in. Shorts for knee or leg issues, a tank top for shoulder or neck work.

What insurance do you take?

Most major commercial plans, plus Medicare, No-Fault, and Workers' Comp. Don't see yours? Call (516) 789-6322 and we'll verify it for you.
