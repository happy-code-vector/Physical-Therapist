After a car accident, the last thing you want to wrestle with is insurance
paperwork while you are hurt. The good news: if you were injured in a crash in
New York, No-Fault coverage generally pays for your physical therapy —
regardless of who caused the accident — and we handle most of the process for
you.

## What No-Fault is

New York is a No-Fault state. Under the No-Fault law, your own auto insurance
carrier generally covers the medical care you need after a car accident — and
that includes physical therapy — no matter who was at fault. The point of the
law is to get injured people into treatment quickly without waiting for a
drawn-out fault determination.

## What it tends to cover

No-Fault commonly covers physical therapy for the injuries we see most after
crashes: whiplash and neck injuries, back pain, and other accident-related
musculoskeletal injuries. Once a claim is open and treatment is authorized, you
typically do not pay out of pocket at the time of your visits. The specifics
depend on your policy and claim, so we verify everything up front.

## Time windows matter

This is the part to take seriously: No-Fault has deadlines for starting care
and filing the claim. Do not wait for the pain to "just go away on its own" —
soft-tissue injuries from a crash often get worse before they get better, and
waiting can complicate both your recovery and your coverage. Get evaluated
promptly. If you are unsure where you stand, call us and we will walk you
through it.

## What we handle for you

We verify your No-Fault coverage, obtain the treatment authorizations, and
manage the billing — so the administrative load stays off your plate. You will
still want to see your physician and get any imaging they recommend; we
coordinate with your care team rather than replacing it. Our job is to get you
moving again while the paperwork runs in the background.
